My research focuses on society-environment relations and linked social-environmental systems, urbanization and industrial transformation, and the relationship between science and policy. I develop original computational, spatial, and mixed methods approaches to create new data from primary sources, while also taking computation and large-scale data as objects of substantive and theoretical investigation.

My work has appeared in *Social Forces*, *City & Community*, and *Social Studies of Science*, among others, and has been supported by the National Institute of Standards and Technology, the Superfund Research Program of the NIEHS, CROCUS (Argonne National Laboratory), and the SEEKCommons Project (NSF award #2226425).
